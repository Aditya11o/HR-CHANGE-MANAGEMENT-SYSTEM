## 1. Executive Summary & Rule Cataloguing Architecture

The University HR Change Management & Automation System is governed by strict institutional policies, regulatory mandates (UGC/Statutory Councils), and administrative hierarchies. To prevent operational ambiguity and eliminate arbitrary system behaviors, this catalogue consolidates every authoritative **Business Rule** and **Decision Point** operating across the institution.

In strict compliance with Phase 2 documentation governance:
- **Zero Technical Invention:** No software implementation patterns (such as database triggers, HTTP status codes, JSON formats, or ORM models) are presented as business rules.
- **Strict Classification Integrity:** Every rule is classified according to the official project framework (`[A] Explicit Requirement`, `[B] Logical Implication`, `[C] Approved Technical Decision`, `[D] Proposed Detail`, or `[E] TBD / Open Decision`).
- **Domain Separation:** Institutional separation between Module II Academic and Non-Academic recruitment tracks, as well as Module III's three independent performance subsystems, is rigorously enforced.

### Rule Taxonomy
- **Section A:** Module I Business Rules (`BR-M1-001` to `BR-M1-008`)
- **Section B:** Module II Recruitment Rules (`BR-M2-001` to `BR-M2-022`)
